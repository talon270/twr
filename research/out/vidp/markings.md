# VIDP markings, signs and lights for a top-down renderer

AAI AD 2.9 (AMDT 10/2026) identifies runway designation, threshold, touchdown-zone, centreline, aiming-point and side-stripe markings; taxiway centreline, side-stripe, intermediate-holding, runway-holding and enhanced-centreline markings; runway guard lights; stop bars; runway-crossing lights; and RETILs. It states that taxiway edge lights are installed on curves only. The general colour/pattern rules below follow ICAO Annex 14 Vol I, Chapter 5. The chart-specific placement of the marks and signs was not read, so this is a renderer reference, not a surveyed layout.

| Element | Colour (ICAO term) | Pattern and dimensions | Where at VIDP / meaning | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Runway designation | White | Two-digit runway number; parallel-runway suffix letters where needed. Exact glyph size depends on runway code and Annex figure. | All eight directions; tells pilot runway direction. | AAI AD 2.9 (runway designation present); ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.2.2 and Figure 5-2 (primary). |
| Threshold | White | Longitudinal threshold stripes, count/width/gap per runway width in Annex Figure 5-3; exact VIDP stripe count not transcribed. | Each usable threshold; marks start of landing distance. | AAI AD 2.9, AMDT 10/2026; ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.2.3/Fig. 5-3 (primary). |
| Runway centreline | White | Dashed longitudinal line; dimensions and spacing per Annex §5.2.6 and Figure 5-5. | Runways; alignment and directional guidance. | AAI AD 2.9, AMDT 10/2026; ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.2.6/Fig. 5-5 (primary). |
| Touchdown zone | White | Paired rectangular bars in groups; exact layout depends on runway length and available landing distance. | AAI lists TDZ markings/lights on 10, 28, 11L, 29R, 11R and 29L. | AAI AD 2.9/2.14, AMDT 10/2026; ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.2.4/Fig. 5-4 (primary). |
| Aiming point | White | Two broad rectangular markings, dimensions/spacing by runway length per Annex Figure 5-4. | All runways per AAI runway-marking list; visual aiming reference. | AAI AD 2.9, AMDT 10/2026; ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.2.5/Fig. 5-4 (primary). |
| Runway edge / side stripe | White | Continuous edge stripes; AAI uses the term runway side stripe. Exact widths per Annex §5.2.7. | Runway edges; delineates paved runway width. | AAI AD 2.9, AMDT 10/2026; ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.2.7 (primary). |
| Taxiway centreline | Yellow | Continuous line at least 15 cm wide; interrupted at runway-holding and intermediate-holding markings. | Paved taxiways; nose-wheel guidance. | AAI AD 2.9, AMDT 10/2026; ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.2.8.10 (primary). |
| Enhanced taxiway centreline | Yellow | Centreline with dashed yellow lines on each side; up to 47 m from a runway-holding position. Figure 5-7 gives 15 cm dashed-line width, 15–30 cm centreline, and 0.9 m spacing details. | AAI confirms VIDP uses enhanced centreline marking, but does not name locations in AD 2.9. | AAI AD 2.9, AMDT 10/2026; ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.2.8.9/Fig. 5-7 (primary). |
| Taxiway edge / side marking | Yellow | Continuous or dashed double lines depending on whether the taxiway edge meets a usable shoulder; exact dimensions per Annex §5.2.9. | AAI says edge lights are only on curves; it lists reflective edge markers on parts of LINK 32, LINK 30, LINK 16, LINK 13 and taxilane F6. | AAI AD 2.9, AMDT 10/2026; ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.2.9 (primary). |
| Runway-holding position, Pattern A | Yellow | Two solid lines on runway side and two dashed lines on taxiway side; exact line/gap dimensions are in Annex Figure 5-8. | At runway entries. Prevents entry/crossing absent clearance. | AAI AD 2.9, AMDT 10/2026; ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.2.10/Figs. 5-6, 5-8 (primary). |
| Runway-holding position, Pattern B | Yellow | Two dashed lines on runway side and two solid lines on taxiway side; dimensions in Annex Figure 5-8. | Farther holding point where multiple runway-holding positions exist; CAT II/III protection. AAI does not enumerate its VIDP instances in AD 2.9. | ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.2.10/Figs. 5-6, 5-8 (primary); VIDP placement unresolved. |
| Intermediate holding position | Yellow | Dashed single line across taxiway; exact dimensions per Annex Figure 5-6. | AAI confirms IHP markings/lights at VIDP, but does not list locations in AD 2.9. Stop/hold limit short of a runway. | AAI AD 2.9, AMDT 10/2026; ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.2.11/Fig. 5-6 (primary). |
| ILS critical / sensitive-area boundary | No general pavement colour/pattern asserted | No generic surface marking specified here; VIDP charted boundaries and limit signage need chart inspection. | Critical area excludes aircraft/vehicles during ILS operations; sensitive-area movements are controlled to protect signal integrity. | AAI AD 2.22, AMDT 10/2026, §§1.3–1.4, 2.4; ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §3.12.9; VIDP AD 2.24 chart listed (primary). |
| Stand lead-in / stop line | Yellow lead-in; stop-line colour/site form unresolved | AAI says continuous lines on MARS centre stands and single-aircraft stands; broken lines on left/right MARS positions for narrowbodies. Nose-wheel stop lines are provided. Exact dimensions and stop-line colour not published in AD 2.9. | Stand approach and nose-wheel stopping point. | AAI AD 2.9, AMDT 10/2026 (primary); exact stand chart/dimensions unresolved. |
| Mandatory instruction sign | Red background, white inscription | Rectangular, runway holding position/runway designation instruction; exact face dimensions per ICAO Annex 14 Table 5-4. | Taxiway/runway entry and other mandatory instruction points; tells pilot to stop or comply. | ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.4.2/Table 5-4 (primary); AAI AD 2.9, AMDT 10/2026 says signs at taxiway intersections, straight sections and holding positions. |
| Location sign | Black background, yellow inscription | Rectangular sign; face dimensions per ICAO Annex 14 table 5-4. | Taxiway intersections; identifies current taxiway or runway location. | AAI AD 2.9, AMDT 10/2026; ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.4.3/Table 5-4 (primary). |
| Direction sign | Yellow background, black inscription and arrow | Rectangular directional face; dimensions per ICAO Annex 14 table 5-4. | Taxiway intersections; indicates route toward named taxiways. | AAI AD 2.9, AMDT 10/2026; ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.4.3/Table 5-4 (primary). |
| Stop bar | Red lights | A row of red lights across taxiway; ICAO spacing/details are in §5.3.20. | AAI lists stop bars at runway holding positions on A2, D1–D9 selected holds, G/H/J/K/Y/Z/P/R/S/T holds and at named taxiway junctions; see AD 2.9. Red means stop. | AAI AD 2.9, AMDT 10/2026; ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.3.20 (primary). |
| RETILs | Yellow lights | Fixed unidirectional yellow lights, spaced 2 m apart; count and longitudinal placement follow Figure 5-25. | AAI lists RETIL exits on 10 (G1/G2/J1/J2/J3), 28 (H1/K1/K2), 11R (Y1/Y2/Y4), 29L (Z2/Z3), 11L (R2/R3), 29R (P1/P3), and 27 (D5/D6). Indicates rapid exit; it does not replace ATC clearance. | AAI AD 2.9/2.14, AMDT 10/2026; ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §§5.3.15.3–5.3.15.5/Fig. 5-25 (primary). |
| Runway guard lights | Alternating yellow lights | Pair of elevated lights or in-pavement row on each side of taxiway/runway intersection per installation type. | AAI says provided at each runway/taxiway intersection; warns of runway entry. | AAI AD 2.9, AMDT 10/2026; ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.3.23 (primary). |
| Runway crossing lights | Alternating yellow lights | In-pavement pairs across taxiway at runway holding position; exact geometry per Annex §5.3.22. | AAI lists runway crossing light pairs at K/H, J/G, D/A2, P/S and R/T intersections (AD 2.9). | AAI AD 2.9, AMDT 10/2026; ICAO Annex 14 Vol I 2022 §5.3.22 (primary). |

**Unresolved renderer dimensions:** exact widths, dash lengths, spacing, glyph sizes and sign face sizes should be taken from the cited Annex figures/tables, not estimated from this summary. ICAO Annex 14 2022 is the version inspected; verify India’s filed differences in AIP GEN 1.7 before treating every recommendation as a VIDP requirement.

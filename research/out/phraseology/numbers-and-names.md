# Numbers and names in the radio log

Use two representations: the typed input keeps compact codes such as `29L`, `118.550` and `A2`; the controller/pilot radio log expands them to spoken forms. ICAO Doc 9432 is the primary authority for number pronunciation, callsigns and phonetic spelling, but its 4th edition (2007) is behind ICAO's current document cycle and is sold through the ICAO Store. AAI MATS Part 1 (Edition 7, 9 May 2025) adopts ICAO procedures. The current eAIP page available on 10 November 2026 is AIRAC 2026-10.

The telephony designator source is the existing [operators.csv](../aircraft/operators.csv), accessed 10 November 2026. It identifies IndiGo as ICAO code IGO, telephony `IFLY`; the game should store those as separate fields. AAI MATS classifies this as a telephony designator followed by flight identification and gives this type “No abbreviated form” (Table 12-8). Do not infer the spoken callsign from the three-letter operator code.
